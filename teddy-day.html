What You Mean to Me

Three promises I'll always keep

01

I Promise to Be Your Safe Place Too

Just as you hold me when I need comfort, I promise to be your shelter. To hold you without judgment, listen without rushing to fix, and love you through every emotion. You'll always have a soft place to land with me.

02

I Promise to Choose Gentleness

In a world that can be harsh, I promise to be gentle with you—with your heart, your hopes, your fears. To speak kindly, act tenderly, and never forget how precious you are to me.

03

I Promise to Hold You Close

Not just physically, but emotionally. To keep you close in my thoughts, my plans, my heart. To never take your presence for granted. You are my favorite person, and I'll never stop reminding you of that.

Just like a teddy bear holds memories of comfort and childhood safety, you hold all my hopes, dreams, and deepest feelings. You've become the one I run to, the one I celebrate with, and the one I choose every single day.

Forever in your arms ♡
