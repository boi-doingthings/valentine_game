Every Kiss I Cherish

Forehead Kisses

The gentle press of your lips on my forehead that says "I've got you" louder than any words could. These kisses feel like protection, like tenderness, like home. They're my favorite goodnight, my sweetest comfort.

Surprise Kisses

The unexpected kiss that catches me off guard and makes me blush. Whether you're sneaking up from behind or stealing one mid-conversation, these kisses remind me that romance lives in spontaneity.

🌊

Deep, Slow Kisses

The kind that makes time stop and the world disappear. Where nothing exists except you, me, and this moment. These kisses speak of passion, connection, and a love that runs deeper than oceans.

Playful Kisses

The quick pecks that make us both laugh. The butterfly kisses. The silly smooches that turn into giggles. These kisses remind me that love doesn't always have to be serious—it can be light, fun, and joyful.
